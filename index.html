01 — THE PROFILEABOUT JUDE

The person
behind the production.

I am a film and television producer with extensive experience in the Indian advertising and media industry, working across advertising films, promotional content, feature films and television productions.

My career has taken me through different formats, production environments and scales — from fast-paced commercial shoots to large-scale feature film and television productions.

I have worked across the complete production process, handling budgets, schedules, locations, crew, vendors, casting, logistics and on-ground execution. My approach is rooted in understanding the creative vision and making it happen practically — without losing sight of time, resources or the details that make a production work.
